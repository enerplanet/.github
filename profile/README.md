# EnerPlanET

Community energy grid planning and simulation platform.

> [!NOTE]
> We are working on making EnerPlanET open-source. Follow this organisation to get updates.

Access the [EnerPlanET website](https://enerplanet.th-deg.de/) for more information.

## What is EnerPlanET?

EnerPlanET lets planners and engineers design, simulate, and optimise local
low-voltage distribution networks, from drawing a polygon on a map to a full
energy optimisation result.

It covers:

- **Synthetic grid generation** using [PYLOVO](https://pylovo.readthedocs.io)
- **Multi-technology energy modelling** based on [NREL-PySAM](https://nrel-pysam.readthedocs.io/en/main/getting-started.html)
- **Energy optimisation** via [Calliope](https://calliope.readthedocs.io/) and [PyPSA](https://pypsa.org/)
- **Interactive 2D/3D geospatial visualisation**
- **Scenario management** for comparing different grid designs and energy mixes
- **Integration with real-world data** (e.g. weather, load profiles)
- **Result dashboard** for analysing energy flows, costs, and emissions
